    About Pushkar

    I edit videos for a living, and I've been doing it for more than five years. Most of that time has been freelance, for over fifty different clients — one week a moody music edit, the next an animated wedding invitation, a talking-head explainer, or a product video that has to sell.

    That range is what I'm proudest of: every brief needs its own pace, its own grade and its own type. I also worked as a video editor at Uniqus Digital Solutions, an ed-tech company, where the job was making educational video clear and watchable.

    Outside client work I run my own YouTube channel and Instagram account about editing, which is where I try new ideas and share what I've learned.
